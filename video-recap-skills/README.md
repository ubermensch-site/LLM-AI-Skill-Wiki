# ZenStory Video Recap Skills

A seven-skill workflow for turning one or more videos into a Chinese narrated recap and an editable CapCut/Jianying draft. It covers video understanding, story planning, clip selection, narration, voiceover, assembly, subtitles, QC, and optional production-reference extraction.

## Why it qualifies

- More than 600 GitHub stars with active releases and CI.
- Maintained by ZenStory AI with a real end-to-end example and machine-readable production/QC artifacts.
- MIT licensed, local-first for media processing, and explicit about API, cost, authorization, and voice-cloning boundaries.

## Key capabilities

- Combine scene detection, ASR, VLM analysis, and timeline evidence.
- Plan audience promise, visual/audio ownership, clip boundaries, and narration.
- Produce mixed audio, subtitles, editable multi-track timelines, and QC reports.
- Resume from cached analysis and keep production decisions auditable.
