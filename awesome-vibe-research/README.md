# Awesome Vibe Research

ModelScope's curated collection of AI-assisted scientific research agents, skills, workflows, tools, and best practices. It maps the research lifecycle from question definition and literature review through data, experiments, visualization, writing, reproducibility, and impact.

## Why it qualifies

- Maintained under the official ModelScope organization with active automated updates.
- Uses explicit inclusion criteria: runnable or reusable workflows, clear documentation, recent maintenance, and practical research examples.
- Connects entries to source repositories and papers, making it useful as a discovery index rather than a prompt dump.

## Key capabilities

- Browse research tooling by lifecycle stage.
- Discover scientific skills, agents, reproducibility workflows, and evidence-backed projects.
- Track stars and link out to demos, papers, and upstream implementations.
